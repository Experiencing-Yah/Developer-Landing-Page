About Hebrew Harvester

You inherit a small farm in the hill country of Israel. Plow, sow, and reap barley, wheat, lentils, melons, and more, and care for your flock of sheep. The year follows the biblical calendar: months begin with the new moon, days begin at sunset, and the seventh day is a day of rest.

When the pilgrimage feasts come, leave your farm in good hands and travel to Jerusalem. Bring your tithes and offerings to the Temple, live in a booth for the Feast of Tabernacles, meet pilgrims from across the land, and play games together. A journal collects the Scriptures behind everything you do.

Features

Seasons of the Land

Sow grain with the early rains, wave the first sheaf of barley in spring, and grow vegetables and lentils in their months.

The Biblical Calendar

Watch for the new moon, prepare on the sixth day, and rest on the Sabbath.

Pilgrimage Feasts

Travel to Jerusalem for Passover, Shavuot, and Sukkot, and live in your booth for all eight days.

Temple Offerings

Bring firstfruits and tithes, and choose between sin offerings and freewill offerings.

Life in the Village

Trade at the market, hire help for your trips, leave the corners of your field for gleaners, and get to know your neighbors.

Sabbath Games

Relax with a collection of mini-games on the Sabbath and during the feasts.
