Frequently Asked Questions

How do I move and do things?
On a phone or tablet, tap a spot to walk there, or touch and drag anywhere to steer. Tap the round Act button to till, water, reap, or talk to whatever you are facing. On a computer, use WASD or the arrow keys to walk and J or Space to act. The full list is under How to Play in the pause menu.
How is my game saved?
Your farm saves itself as you play, and you can also save from the pause menu. The save is kept only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your farm.
How do I start over?
From the title screen, tap New Game and confirm. This replaces your current farm and cannot be undone.
Why can't I work on the Sabbath?
The game follows the biblical week. From sunset on the sixth day to sunset on the seventh, fields and shops rest. Prepare food for yourself and your flock on the sixth day, then spend the Sabbath resting, visiting, or playing the Sabbath games at home.
How do I travel to Jerusalem?
Walk through the village of Bethlehem to the road on its far side. You can go up for any pilgrimage feast. At other times you'll need a lamb or enough silver for an offering, and someone hired to care for your animals while you're away.
When do months and feasts begin?
Each month begins when the new moon is sighted, and each day begins at sunset. Open the calendar to see the current month, the moon phases, and the coming appointed times.
Where do the Scripture references come from?
Every lesson in the journal cites the passages it draws on, mostly from the Torah, so you can read them for yourself.
Does the game need the internet?
No. Everything is included in the app, and it plays fully offline.
