Hebrew Harvester Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

App Name: Hebrew Harvester
Developer: ExperiencingYah.com (Think Transformation, Inc.)
Platforms: iPhone, iPad, and Android

This privacy policy describes how Hebrew Harvester ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your data. Hebrew Harvester is a farming game set in ancient Israel. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

In short: Hebrew Harvester does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

Hebrew Harvester stores the following data locally on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • Your saved game (your farm, inventory, and progress)
  • Settings such as sound, music, and text speed preferences
  • Best scores from the mini-games

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

No Network Use

All of the game's art, music, and text are included in the app. The game does not connect to the internet while you play, and it does not require an account or sign-in.

No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Hebrew Harvester does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking, and it contains no in-app purchases.

Beta Testing

If you join the beta, Apple (TestFlight) or Google (Google Play testing) may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in their own privacy policies. If you sign up for the beta on our website, we use your name and email only to send your invitation and updates about the test.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For privacy-related questions: https://ExperiencingYah.com
